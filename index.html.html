How scoring works

Connections matter

🟡 1 shared trait🟢 2–3 shared traits⚪ No synergy
⚡ Same type +20🌎 Same generation +15🔁 Same evolution stage +15

Each Pokémon adds its tier power to your score: S 100 · A 80 · B 60 · C 40 · D 20. Its actual BST is shown for reference. Two Pokémon earn synergy only when connected by a visible line. Bonuses stack if they match in multiple categories. Dual-type Pokémon match if they share at least one type.

The Ace connects to all five outer positions. Outer Pokémon connect only to their two immediate neighbors around the ring. Numbered outer slots run 1–5 clockwise from the top. You may choose the Ace at any time, but once it is placed, it cannot be moved or replaced. Select any outer Pokémon to move or swap it with another outer slot until you lock in your completed team.

Lines update immediately: yellow = 1 shared category; green = 2 or 3 shared categories. A gray solid line means the Pokémon are connected without a match.
